About Amihan Escapes

A Palawan tour operator built around the calm, dry season and the people who live here.

Our story

Amihan Escapes started as a weekend side project of three friends from Puerto Princesa who kept getting the same question from visitors: where should we go and how do we get there? We began by writing simple itineraries, then partnered with boat owners and guides we trusted.

Today we organize island hopping, lagoon tours, river cave trips and multi-day packages across northern and central Palawan, with a focus on small groups and fair pay for local boat crews.

Mission

To make Palawan easy to plan and good for the communities that host visitors, by offering honest information, fair prices and low-impact tours.

Vision

To be the first name travelers think of when they want a well-organized, responsible Palawan trip.

Why Palawan matters for tourism

World-class nature

Palawan is known for limestone cliffs, coral reefs and the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Livelihood for communities

Boat crews, guides, homestays and food sellers depend on visitors, especially in El Nido and Coron.

A fragile environment

Reefs and lagoons need care. Visitor limits, environmental fees and reef-safe habits help protect them.

Who we serve

Target market: Filipino and international leisure travelers aged 22 to 45, including couples, friend groups and young families, who want a planned trip with local guides and clear pricing.

Our advantage

  • One inquiry covers boats, vans, guides and hotel suggestions
  • Maximum of 12 guests per boat for a calmer experience
  • Itineraries timed to avoid the busiest lagoon hours
  • Clear inclusions, exclusions and fee reminders before you book
