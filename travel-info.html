Travel Information & Tourist Guide

Practical tips to plan, pack and travel well in Palawan.

Best time to visit

November to May is the dry season, with calm seas and the best visibility. The Amihan months of November to March are cooler. June to October is the wet season, with more rain and occasional tour cancellations.

Weather

Expect 26 to 33 degrees Celsius most of the year. Boat tours can be canceled when the coast guard suspends sea travel. Choose flexible bookings and keep a spare day when possible.

What to bring

  • Swimwear, quick-dry shirt and light cover-up
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, hat and sunglasses
  • Dry bag and phone pouch
  • Water shoes for rocky landings
  • Cash, since many islands have no ATMs
  • Power bank and a copy of your booking

Dress and etiquette

Beachwear is fine on boats and beaches. Cover up in town, churches and restaurants. Greet people with a smile, say po and opo to elders, and ask before photographing locals.

Environmental guidelines

  • Do not touch or stand on corals
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen only
  • Take all trash back to the boat
  • Do not feed fish or wildlife
  • Pay the required environmental fees

Safety and accessibility

Wear life vests on the boat and listen to the crew. Some lagoons need short climbs or wading, so tell us about mobility needs early and we will suggest easier routes such as Honda Bay or beach days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book the Underground River in advance?

Yes. Entry is by permit and daily numbers are limited. We reserve it for you once your dates are confirmed.

What happens if my tour is canceled by bad weather?

When sea travel is suspended, we reschedule you to another day if possible. If not, we refund the unused tour portion according to our booking terms.

Can children join?

Yes. Honda Bay and Nacpan are good for children. Ask us about life vest sizes and age limits for each boat tour.

How do I pay?

For this academic project no payment is collected on the site. In a real operation we would confirm payment options by email after your inquiry.

Is the 10% Amihan Season offer combinable?

It cannot be combined with other discounts and applies to the tour price, not airfare or fees.
