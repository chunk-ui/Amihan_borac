Coron Wreck Snorkel

  • From PHP 1,500
  • Half day
  • Daily

Coron Bay holds Japanese vessels sunk in 1944. Shallower wrecks can be seen by snorkelers; deeper ones are for certified divers.

Stay and eat

Accommodation

El Nido has beach cottages, hostels and mid-range resorts around the town and Corong-Corong. Coron town has budget inns and hotels near the harbor. Puerto Princesa has city hotels and eco-lodges. We suggest options when you inquire.

Dining

Try grilled seafood, kinilaw, tuna sisig and fresh coconut. Many boat lunches include grilled fish, rice and fruit.

Nearby attractions

  • Taraw Cliff viewpoint, El Nido
  • Mount Tapyas, Coron
  • Maquinit Hot Springs, Coron
  • Baker's Hill and Iwahig farm, Puerto Princesa
