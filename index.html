Why we are called Amihan

Amihan is the Filipino word for the cool northeast monsoon wind that blows from about November to March. In Palawan it also marks the dry, calm season when the sea is flat and the lagoons are at their best.

We are a Puerto Princesa-based tour operator for first-time visitors, couples, friends and families. Every package includes a local guide, boat transfers and an itinerary that leaves room to swim and rest.
